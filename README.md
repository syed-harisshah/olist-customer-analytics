# Olist Customer Analytics
### Retail Customer Segmentation & Retention Analysis

---

## Who built this and why

I'm Haris — a Pakistani-born retail operator turned data analyst, 
currently completing my MBAN at UBC Sauder.

Before grad school I co-owned The Mean Girls Store, a western women's 
clothing brand. I spent years making inventory, pricing, and customer 
decisions mostly on instinct and experience. Good instincts get you 
far — but I kept hitting the same wall: I couldn't quantify which

margin, and when exactly a customer had stopped caring about us.

That's what this project is about. I built it to develop the technical

drives retention strategy at companies like Lululemon and Aritzia.

---

## The dataset

**Olist Brazilian E-Commerce** — 100,000 real orders from 2016–2018
across a marketplace of 3,000+ sellers and 96,000+ customers.

No equivalent Canadian transaction-level dataset exists publicly.
The techniques here — RFM segmentation, churn modeling, CLV prediction 
— transfer directly to what Vancouver retailers run on their internal data.

Dataset: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce
